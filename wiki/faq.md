# FAQ

Short answers to common questions.

## I hit someone but nothing happened. Why?

They were probably **rolling** (attacks pass through a roll) or had just respawned and were
**spawn protected** (they shimmer). See [Combat](combat.md#the-roll-dodge).

## Why did I die in one hit?

Either someone hit you from behind with a heavy (an [execute](combat.md#execute)), or they
held the [Golden Knife](match-rules.md#the-golden-knife). The killfeed says which.

## How do I heal?

Avoid being hit for 3 seconds, then you heal 1 health a second. See
[Healing](combat.md#healing).

## How do I play with friends?

One of you creates a private room and shares the 5-letter code (it's in the pause menu); the
others type it under **Join with a code**. See [Playing online](getting-started.md#playing-online).

## My controller isn't doing anything.

Press any button on it once so the game switches to it. See
[Controllers](controls.md#controllers).

## Can I change the keys?

Yes: **Settings → Controls**. See [Changing your controls](controls.md#changing-your-controls).

## The music is too loud or too quiet.

**Settings → Sound → Music**. See [Sound](settings.md#sound).

## Does practice count?

No. Only online matches go into match history and the leaderboard.
