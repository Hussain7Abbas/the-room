# Getting started

Everything from opening the game to finishing your first match.

![The main menu, with the room list in the middle](images/main-menu.jpg)

## Your name and character

At the top right of the main menu, type the name other players will see and pick your
character. Both are remembered for next time. Zain is the only character for now.

## Practice alone

**Practice alone** drops you into the arena with nobody else. Nothing you do there counts
towards the leaderboard. It's the best place to learn the [controls](controls.md) and try
the [roll](combat.md#the-roll-dodge) and your [ability](abilities.md).

## Playing online

The **Play** page lists the open rooms, refreshed every 5 seconds.

- **Join a room:** select it and press **Join room**, or double-click it.
- **Join a friend's private room:** type the 5-letter code under **Join with a code**.
- **Create a room:** give it a name, pick a [mode](match-rules.md#modes), tick **Private** if
  only people with the code should get in, and press **Create & join**. The code appears in
  the pause menu so you can share it. A room closes after 5 minutes with nobody in it.

## Your first match

1. You spawn in the lane along the arena walls, briefly shimmering: that's
   [spawn protection](combat.md#dying-and-respawning).
2. Head for the middle. Fights happen around the [plaza](arena.md#plaza).
3. Every kill scores points. The first player to the score target, or the leader when the
   8-minute clock runs out, wins. See [Match rules](match-rules.md).
4. After the results screen a new match starts by itself. Stay for the next round, or press
   Esc → **Leave room**.

> **Tip:** hold **Tab** at any time to see the scoreboard.

## Match history and leaderboard

**Match history** lists finished matches with the full scores; select one for its details.
**Leaderboard** ranks players across all matches. Both have pages you can step through.
