# Abilities

Every character has one special ability on a cooldown. Press **E** (Y on Xbox, Triangle on
PlayStation) to use it.

## How abilities work

- Every ability starts with a **tell**: a visible wind-up of at least 0.3 s, so the other
  player always has a chance to react.
- After use it goes on **cooldown**. The ability tile at the top left of your screen counts it
  down and glows gold when it's ready again.
- Every ability can be countered. Its counter is listed below.

## Zain: Drop Kick

| | |
|---|---|
| Tell | 0.45 s leap (the kick animation) |
| Effect | Lunges up to 3.5 m and hits whoever is straight ahead with both feet |
| Damage | 70 (twice a light attack), and it staggers |
| Cooldown | 7 s |
| Counter | Roll through it, or step aside: it only hits straight ahead |

> **Tip:** a light attack (35) followed by a Drop Kick (70) kills from full health.

## More characters

Each new character is designed by the Voidra developer it's based on, with an ability that
follows the same rules. They'll appear in the character picker when they're ready.
