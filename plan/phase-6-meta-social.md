# Phase 6 — Meta & Social Layer (post-M4)

**Goal:** the things that carry across sessions — status, not power (GDD §4 meta loop, §7).

**Gate question:** *Do people rematch unprompted and re-tell moments afterwards?*

## Tasks
- [ ] Server-side persistence (SQLite or JSON on the VPS): per-player season stats, award counts.
- [ ] Season leaderboard (in lobby).
- [ ] Results webhook → team chat (Discord/Slack/Teams — whichever the team uses) posting the match summary + awards.
- [ ] Cosmetic unlocks tied to award milestones: knife skins, victory poses, custom voice lines. **Zero mechanical effect.**
- [ ] Lobby: banter space, mode/config select, character select.
- [ ] Remaining characters added as they pass review (up to ~20).

## Explicitly never
XP, stat upgrades, unlockable abilities, currencies, loot boxes, random drops, daily login rewards, real-clock timers (GDD §4, §7, §8).

## Later modes (only once Deathmatch is fun)
Team DM, Last-Man-Standing, Golden Knife Rush.
