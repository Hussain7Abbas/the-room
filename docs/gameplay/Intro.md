# Gameplay

Everyone spawns into one room with one knife and one character ability. You score by killing,
more by killing players who are on a streak, and the first player to the score target (or the
leader when time runs out) wins. Matches restart automatically after an 8 s results screen.

| Mode | Players | Score target |
|---|---|---|
| Duel Pit | 6–10 | 25 |
| Chaos | 12–20 | 40 |

Match time limit: 8 minutes. See [Match loop](../architecture/match-loop.md) for bounty, the Golden
Knife and Last Call.
